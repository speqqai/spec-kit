# Research spec template

A research spec answers a question. It says what you need to know, how you will find out, and what you learned. It is a living document: write the question and approach before the work, the findings and recommendation after. Keep every part to a few plain sentences. Follow `../references/style-guide.md` for the writing.

## Overview (page tab)

Write the first parts before the work, the last parts after.

1. **Question.** The one thing this research must answer, in a line, and why it matters now.
2. **What we know.** The current understanding, and where it is thin or uncertain.
3. **Approach.** How you will find out: the sources, the code, the experiments, the people to ask.
4. **Findings.** What you learned, as you learn it. Facts first, with where each one came from.
5. **Recommendation.** What to do about it, and the confidence behind it. The decision this research unblocks.
